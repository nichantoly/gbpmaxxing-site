How Google picks the three businesses on the map

Google publishes exactly one document on how local ranking works, and it names three things. Every consultant's "proprietary framework" is a theory about these three words.

Relevance

Do you match the search? Google reads your categories, services, description, reviews and the website behind your profile to decide what you actually do. You control this almost entirely.

Chapters 7, 10, 11 and 18

Distance

How close you are to the person searching. You can't change it, and nobody else can either. Strong relevance and prominence widen your reach at the edges. Anyone promising to make you rank everywhere in the metro is selling physics violations.

Chapter 2

Prominence

How known and trusted you are: review count, quality and recency, plus mentions and links around the web. It compounds, it's slow, and a new competitor can't fake it. That's why it's worth building.
