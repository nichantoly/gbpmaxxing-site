Changelog

The guide gets updated when Google changes. This page shows what moved in each version, so you know if your copy is current.

Version 1.4 November 5, 2026

Google shipped more change in the three months before this edition than in the year before it. New and changed claims were checked against primary sources on November 5, 2026. This is the first edition published on gbpmaxxing.com.

Two words: do this.

Start Here, Action section

Upload the PDF to your AI and type "do this." The guide now tells your AI what that means, so anyone can run the whole build without writing a prompt.

AI became the front door.

Chapters 1, 19, 20

45% of consumers used AI tools for local business recommendations in BrightLocal's 2026 survey, up from 6% the year before. Google's own agents now call businesses, book appointments, and answer your texts.

Google's October 6 policy update.

Chapters 10, 11, 15, 16

No time-bound promotions, pricing, or direct calls-to-action in listing content. No phone numbers, emails, or social links in post text.

The four-day clock.

Chapters 6, 22

Google now gives owners four days to reject certain suggested edits before it may publish them.

Collected info.

Chapters 20, 22

Your profile now shows what Google gathered about you, and Google confirms details by automated call, text, or WhatsApp.
